## Protecting Your Ideas

### Before Sharing Anything:

1. **Qualify the Lead**
   - Real budget? Real timeline?
   - Decision maker or tire kicker?
   - LinkedIn profile checks out?

2. **Start Broad, Get Specific**
   - First call: Capabilities overview
   - Second call: Project concepts
   - Third call: Technical details

3. **Use NDAs When Needed**
   - Free template: https://www.docracy.com/
   - Required for: Investors, partners, competitors

4. **Watermark Everything**
   - Add their email to mockups
   - Timestamp on PDFs
   - Track document views

5. **Trust Your Gut**
   - If something feels off, don't share
   - Ideas are valuable
   - You can always share more later
